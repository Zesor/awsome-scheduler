2.0.0Latest
24 October 2026

The new CalendarKit: rebuilt on a framework-free engine, redesigned, accessible and ready for phones.

Highlights
  • No setup. The calendar ships its own compiled, scoped stylesheet: no Tailwind configuration, nothing to import.
  • Redesigned. Three themes (Calm, Vivid, Editorial) in light and dark, every colour a CSS variable.
  • Create in place. Click or drag over free time; the quick card understands sentences in English and French.
  • Three layouts. Classic, Focus and Flow.
  • … the rest of the highlights, as in CHANGELOG.md
Removed
  • The root cn export, deprecated in 1.4.1.

1.4.0
17 October 2026
Added
  • All-day and multi-day events: continuous bars in the month view, and a collapsible all-day row in the week and day views.
  • Drag a bar to move it by whole days or stretch its right edge; click the all-day row to create an all-day event.
Fixed
  • Multi-day and all-day events no longer appear only on their first day in the month view.
